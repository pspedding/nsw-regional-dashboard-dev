## Executive Summary

This report compares observed SA2 indicators from four Dropbox files retrieved on 28 October 2026. These indicators do not share one reference date: census measures are from 2021, crime from December 2025, and some prices and benefits from September 2026. Rankings are descriptive and use available observations, not causal or population-adjusted comparisons.

1. The 2021 Total Population column sums to **354,803** across 30 SA2s; this is not a current population estimate.
2. Highest house-price observation: **Erina - Green Point** ($1,939,500) (September 2026).

5. Highest assault rate: **Wyong** (12.36 per 1,000) (December 2025).

## 8. Key Challenges & Opportunities

- Challenge: Older census and crime reference periods are not interchangeable with the September 2026 market data.
- Challenge: Raw benefit and business counts require denominator checks before interpreting relative need.
- Opportunity: The latest housing and JobSeeker observations can guide local follow-up, subject to verification of source definitions.
- Opportunity: Quarterly series through June 2026 can be examined alongside, not substituted for, current cross-sectional values.
